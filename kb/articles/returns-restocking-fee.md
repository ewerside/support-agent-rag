title: Is there a restocking fee?
category: returns
---

Only for opened espresso machines and grinders. If you return one in working order after opening it, we charge a 15% restocking fee.

## When the fee applies

The 15% fee applies when all of these are true:

- the item is an espresso machine or a grinder
- the box has been opened
- the item works and is not faulty

We take the fee from your refund, so you don't pay anything extra.

## When there is no fee

- Unopened items, still sealed in their box
- Accessories, coffee and every other item
- Items that arrived faulty or damaged

## Why we charge it

An opened machine can't be sold as new. We test it, clean it and repack it, then sell it as open box at a lower price.

## Used grinders

A grinder that has had beans through it can't be returned at all, with or without a fee. See "Which items can't be returned?".

## How to avoid the fee

If you are not sure a machine is right for you, ask us before you open it. Our team can help you compare models, and an unopened machine can go back with no fee.
