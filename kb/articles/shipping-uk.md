Standard shipping in the UK costs £4.95 and takes 2 to 3 business days. Express shipping costs £9.95 and arrives the next business day.

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 2 to 3 business days | £4.95 |
| Express | Next business day | £9.95 |

UK orders ship from our warehouse in Leeds. For express to arrive the next business day, the order has to leave that day, so order before 1 pm UK time on a business day.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over £60. Express shipping always has a charge.

## Customs and VAT

UK orders never go through customs, because they ship from inside the UK. Prices in the UK store are in pounds and already include VAT.

## Which carrier do you use?

Royal Mail delivers standard orders and DPD delivers express orders. Your tracking email has the link to follow your parcel.

## Northern Ireland, the Highlands and the islands

We ship to every UK address, including Northern Ireland, the Scottish Highlands and the islands. Delivery to these areas can take a little longer than the times above.
