Contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier. Before you do, a few quick checks find most missing parcels.

## If the parcel is marked as delivered

- Look around the delivery spot, including porches, side doors, garages and mailrooms.
- Ask neighbors and anyone else at the address.
- Check the tracking page for a delivery photo or a note about where it was left.
- Wait until the end of the next business day. Carriers sometimes mark a parcel as delivered before it arrives.

## If the parcel is late

Delivery times start when the parcel leaves the warehouse and count business days only. Weekends and public holidays don't count, so a parcel sent on a Friday can look late when it isn't.

If the delivery date in your tracking has passed and the tracking hasn't moved, contact us.

## What we do next

Send us your order number and the email you used at checkout. We contact the carrier, and if the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or refund you in full.

You don't need to wait for the carrier's investigation to finish before we help.

## The tracking says the parcel is coming back to us

This usually means the address was incomplete or nobody could sign for it. When the parcel reaches our warehouse, we refund the order or send it again, whichever you prefer.
