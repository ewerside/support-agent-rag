title: Can you repair my machine after the warranty ends?
category: warranty
---

Yes. We repair machines and grinders that are out of warranty for a diagnostic fee of $49 in the US, C$65 in Canada or £39 in the UK.

## How the fee works

The diagnostic fee covers testing the machine and finding the fault. We then email you a quote for the repair.

If you go ahead, the fee is credited toward the repair. If you don't, you only pay the fee and we send the machine back.

## Shipping

You pay for shipping the machine to us. Pack it well, with the water tank and drip tray empty, and keep the portafilter and accessories at home.

## How long it takes

Repairs take 10 business days from the day the machine reaches us. We email you when the repair is done and the machine is on its way back.

## How to start a repair

Send us a message from the Contact us page with your serial number and a description of the problem. We reply with the address to send the machine to.

## Is my machine still under warranty?

Machines and grinders have a warranty of 2 years from delivery. If yours is newer than that, make a warranty claim instead, and the repair is free. See "How do I make a warranty claim?".
