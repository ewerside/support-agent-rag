title: Which payment methods do you accept?
category: payments
---

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Wrenfield gift cards. Discover is accepted on US orders only.

## Cards

Visa, Mastercard and American Express work in all three stores. Discover works in the US store only.

## Wallets

PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay appear at checkout when your device or browser supports them.

## Gift cards

Enter the gift card code at checkout. If the gift card doesn't cover the full total, pay the rest with a card or a wallet.

## Currency

Each store charges in its own currency: US dollars in the US, Canadian dollars in Canada and pounds in the UK. Your bank may add a fee if your card is in another currency.

## Methods we don't accept

We don't accept cash on delivery, checks, bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

## When we charge you

We charge your payment method when you place the order. For subscriptions, we charge each time a bag ships.
