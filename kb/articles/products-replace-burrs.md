Replace the burrs of your Wrenfield grinder after about 300 kg (660 lb) of coffee, which is about 3 years of daily home use. The swap takes about 15 minutes.

## What you need

- A new burr set from the Parts section of our store
- The 2.5 mm hex key that comes with the grinder
- A brush and a vacuum

## Steps

1. Run the grinder empty so no beans are left, then unplug it and remove the hopper.
2. Turn the dial to the coarsest setting and lift off the top burr carrier.
3. Use the 2.5 mm hex key to remove the screws that hold the top burr. Keep the screws.
4. Remove the bottom burr the same way.
5. Brush and vacuum the grinding chamber.
6. Fit the new burrs. Tighten the screws evenly, in a cross pattern, so each burr sits flat.
7. Put the burr carrier and the hopper back.
8. Calibrate the grinder before you use it. See "How to calibrate your grinder for espresso".

## Signs your burrs are dull

- Grinding takes longer than it used to
- You need a much finer setting for the same shot
- More fine dust and clumps in the grounds

## Warranty

Burrs are wear parts, so the warranty doesn't cover them. See "Are gaskets, burrs and other wear parts covered by the warranty?".
