title: How to calibrate your grinder for espresso
category: products
---

Find the zero point of your Wrenfield grinder first, then start at setting 8 for espresso. Aim for 18 g of coffee in and 36 g out in 25 to 30 seconds.

## Find the zero point

1. Run the grinder empty.
2. While it runs, turn the dial finer, slowly, until you hear the burrs just touch. It sounds like a light chirp.
3. Stop the grinder. Loosen the dial screw, turn the dial ring so it reads 0 and tighten the screw.

Do this after you replace the burrs, or if you can't grind fine enough for espresso.

## Dial in your first shot

1. Set the grinder to setting 8.
2. Grind 18 g of coffee into the portafilter, tamp and start the shot.
3. Weigh the shot as it runs. Stop at 36 g and check the time.

## Adjust the grind

- The shot runs fast, under the target time: go finer by one step.
- The shot runs slow, over the target time: go coarser by one step.
- Change only the grind, not the dose, until the time is right.

## Tips

- Very fresh or older beans may need a different setting from the one you used last time.
- Weigh both the coffee and the shot with a scale. Guessing makes it hard to tell what changed.
- Purge a little coffee after each change, so the next shot uses the new setting.
