title: Do your prices include tax?
category: payments
---

It depends on your country. UK prices include tax, while Canadian taxes and duties and US sales tax are added at checkout.

## United Kingdom

UK prices include 20% VAT. Orders ship from our warehouse in Leeds, so there are no import duties either. The price you see is the price you pay, plus shipping if it applies.

## Canada

Prices in the Canadian store don't include tax. We collect duties and GST/HST at checkout, so there is nothing to pay on delivery.

## United States

Prices in the US store don't include sales tax. We add it at checkout based on your shipping address. Some states don't charge sales tax.

## Do I get a tax invoice?

Yes. Your order confirmation email works as a receipt, and you can download an invoice from Account > Orders. UK invoices show the VAT amount.

## Tax-exempt orders

We don't support tax-exempt orders online. Contact us before you order and we will check what we can do.
