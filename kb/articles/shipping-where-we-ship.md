We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. We don't ship to any other country yet.

## Where your order ships from

US and Canadian orders ship from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio. UK orders ship from our warehouse in Leeds, so they never cross a border.

## When your order leaves the warehouse

Orders placed before 1 pm local warehouse time on a business day leave the same day. Orders placed after that, or on a weekend or public holiday, leave on the following business day.

Local warehouse time is Eastern Time for US and Canadian orders and UK time for UK orders. You get an email with a tracking number as soon as the parcel is on its way.

## Delivery times and costs

Delivery time and cost depend on your country and the shipping speed you pick at checkout. See the article for your country:

- How much does shipping cost in the US?
- How much does shipping to Canada cost?
- How much does shipping cost in the UK?

## Can I ship to an address in another country?

No. The shipping address has to be in one of the three countries we ship to. If you see an error about your address at checkout, this is usually the reason.

## Can I order from one country and ship to another?

Each country has its own store with its own prices, taxes and currency. Use the store for the country you are shipping to, and the checkout will show the right shipping options.
