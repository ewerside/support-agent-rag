id: subscription-cancel
title: How do I cancel my coffee subscription?
category: subscription
---

Go to Account > Subscriptions and click Cancel subscription. There is no fee to cancel.

## Timing

Cancel at least 48 hours before your next ship date. If you cancel later than that, the next bag still ships and is charged, and the subscription ends after it.

## Would a pause work better?

If you have too much coffee or are going away, you can pause for up to 3 months instead. You keep your schedule and your coffee choice, and it restarts on its own. See "How do I skip a delivery or pause my subscription?".

## After you cancel

- You get an email confirming the cancellation.
- Bags that already shipped are not affected.
- You can start a new subscription at any time from the store.

## Can't see the Cancel button?

If you checked out as a guest, you need an account to manage a subscription. Create an account with the same email you used at checkout and the subscription appears in Account > Subscriptions.

You can also contact us and we will cancel it for you.
