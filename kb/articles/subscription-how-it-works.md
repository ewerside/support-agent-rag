category: subscription
---

You get a 340 g bag of fresh coffee every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. Subscription bags cost 10% less than one-off bags and always ship free.

## How to start

1. Open any coffee in the store and choose Subscribe.
2. Pick how often you want it: every 2, 3 or 4 weeks.
3. Pick whole bean or a grind size.
4. Check out. Your first bag ships with your order.

## Billing

We charge your card each time a bag ships, not up front. You get a reminder email a few days before each ship date.

## Managing your subscription

Everything is in Account > Subscriptions. You can change the coffee, skip a delivery, pause or cancel there. See "How do I skip a delivery or pause my coffee subscription?" and "How do I cancel my coffee subscription?".

## Discount codes

The subscription discount can't be combined with discount codes.

## Where we ship subscriptions

Subscriptions are available in the US, Canada and the UK, from the store for your country.

## Freshness

We roast every subscription bag in the week it ships. The roast date is printed on the bag.
