title: How do I get free shipping?
category: shipping
---

Standard shipping is free when your order is over $75 in the US, over C$100 in Canada or over £60 in the UK. The free option appears at checkout once your cart passes the amount for your country.

## How the total is counted

We count the order total after discounts and before tax. If a discount code takes your total below the amount, standard shipping is charged again.

Shipping costs themselves don't count toward the total.

## Is express shipping ever free?

No. Express shipping always has a charge, even on large orders. Pick standard shipping to get the free option.

## Coffee subscriptions

Subscription deliveries always ship free, whatever the order total. See "How does the coffee subscription work?" for the details.

## Why don't I see free shipping at checkout?

Check these first:

- Your total after discounts is at or below the amount for your country.
- You picked express shipping.
- Your shipping address is in a different country from the store you are using.

If none of these fit, contact us before you pay and we will check your cart.

## Can I get a refund on shipping if I add items later?

No. Free shipping is worked out per order, so two smaller orders can't be added together after checkout. If you want to add items, cancel the first order while you still can and place one larger order.
