title: How to backflush your espresso machine
category: products
---

Backflush the group head once a week with plain water and once a month with cleaning powder. It clears coffee oils from the group head and keeps your shots tasting clean.

## What you need

- The blind basket that comes with your machine, which is a basket with no holes
- Wrenfield espresso cleaning powder for the monthly clean

## Weekly backflush with water

1. Put the blind basket in the portafilter and lock it into the group head.
2. Start a shot, let the pump run for a few seconds, then stop it. Water flows back through the group and out into the drip tray.
3. Repeat a few times.
4. Remove the portafilter and run water through the group to rinse it.

## Monthly backflush with cleaning powder

1. Put a small scoop of cleaning powder in the blind basket.
2. Lock in the portafilter and repeat the start and stop cycle until the water in the drip tray looks clean.
3. Remove the blind basket, rinse it and put your normal basket back.
4. Pull one shot and throw it away to clear any taste of the cleaner.

## Signs you need to backflush more often

- A bitter or stale taste in your shots
- Brown residue around the shower screen
- Slow drips from the group head after a shot
