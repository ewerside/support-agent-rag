id: payments-price-match
title: Do you match prices?
category: payments
---

Yes, in two ways. If our price drops within 14 days of your order, we refund the difference. Before you order, we match a lower price from an authorized retailer in your country.

## If our price drops after you order

Contact us with your order number within 14 days of your order. We refund the difference to your original payment method. This also applies to our own sales.

## Matching another store

Before you place your order, send us a link to the lower price. We match it if:

- the store is an authorized retailer in your country
- the item is the same model and color
- the item is in stock at that store

Once we confirm, we send you a code for that price.

## What we don't match

- Marketplace sellers
- Clearance sales
- Used or open box items
- Bundles and free gift offers

## After you order from another store

We can't match a price after you have bought the item somewhere else. The price match is for orders you place with us.
