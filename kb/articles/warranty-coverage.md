category: warranty
---

Espresso machines and grinders have a warranty of 2 years. Accessories have a warranty of 1 year.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in parts and workmanship. For example, a pump that stops working, a heating element that fails or a grinder motor that won't start.

## What is not covered

- Wear parts such as gaskets and burrs. They wear out with normal use.
- Damage from scale buildup. Descale your machine on schedule, see "How to descale your espresso machine".
- Damage from drops, misuse or repairs by someone other than us.

## When it starts

The warranty starts on the delivery date, not the order date. Keep your order confirmation email, or find the order later in Account > Orders.

## What counts as an accessory

Tampers, scales, kettles, milk pitchers, knock boxes and cleaning tools are accessories. Espresso machines and grinders are the only items with the longer warranty.

## How to use it

See "How do I make a warranty claim?" for the steps. If the warranty has ended, we can still repair your machine for a fee, see "Can you repair my machine after the warranty ends?".
