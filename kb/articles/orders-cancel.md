id: orders-cancel
title: How do I cancel my order?
category: orders
---

You can cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it. Go to Account > Orders, open the order and click Cancel order.

## Cancelling within 2 hours

If you are inside the window, the order is cancelled right away and you get a confirmation email. The refund goes back to your original payment method, and your bank may take a few days to show it.

If you checked out as a guest, open the link in your order confirmation email to reach the same page.

## After 2 hours

After 2 hours the order is with the warehouse and can't be cancelled. Once it arrives, you can return it like any other order. See "How do I return an item?" for the steps.

## Can support cancel it for me?

Support can cancel an order for you within the same 2 hours. After that, they can't stop it either, because the parcel is already being packed.

## Cancelling part of an order

You can't remove a single item from an order. Cancel the whole order within 2 hours and place a new one with the items you want.

## Subscriptions

Cancelling a coffee subscription works differently. See "How do I cancel my coffee subscription?".
