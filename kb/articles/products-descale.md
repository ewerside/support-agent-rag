title: How to descale your espresso machine
category: products
---

Descale your Wrenfield espresso machine every 2 months, or every month if you have hard water. The descaling cycle takes about 25 minutes.

## What you need

- 1 sachet of Wrenfield descaler
- 1 liter of water
- A container that holds at least 1 liter
- A cloth

## Steps

1. Empty the drip tray. If you use a water filter, take it out of the tank.
2. Mix 1 sachet of descaler in 1 liter of water and pour it into the water tank.
3. Put the container under the group head and the steam wand.
4. Turn the machine on, then press and hold the Descale button until the light blinks.
5. The machine runs the solution through the group head and the steam wand. This takes about 25 minutes. Empty the container if it gets full.
6. When the light stops blinking, rinse the tank, fill it with clean water and press Descale once to run the rinse cycle.
7. Put the water filter back.

## Why it matters

Hard water leaves scale inside the boiler and pipes. Scale slows the flow, changes the brewing temperature and can damage the machine. Damage from scale buildup isn't covered by the warranty.

## Signs it's time

- Shots run slower than usual at the same grind
- The steam wand is weaker
- White flakes in the drip tray
