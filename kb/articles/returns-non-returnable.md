id: returns-non-returnable
title: Which items can't be returned?
category: returns
---

Gift cards, opened bags of coffee and used grinders can't be returned. Everything else can go back within the return window.

## Gift cards

Gift cards can't be returned or exchanged for cash. If a gift card code doesn't work, contact us and we will check it.

## Coffee

Opened bags of coffee can't be returned, for food safety reasons. Unopened bags can be returned like any other item.

If a bag tastes wrong or arrived damaged, contact us anyway. We will replace it.

## Used grinders

A grinder counts as used once beans have gone through it. Coffee oils and grounds stay inside the burrs, so we can't sell it again.

If you only opened the box and didn't grind anything, you can still return it. A restocking fee applies, see "Is there a restocking fee?".

## Faulty items are different

These rules are for change-of-mind returns. If an item is faulty or arrived damaged, we take care of it no matter what it is. See "How do I make a warranty claim?" or "My order arrived damaged".
