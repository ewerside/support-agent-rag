title: How do I skip a delivery or pause my coffee subscription?
category: subscription
---

Go to Account > Subscriptions. You can skip the next delivery or pause the subscription for up to 3 months.

## Skip a delivery

1. Open Account > Subscriptions.
2. Click Skip next delivery.
3. Your next bag moves to the following date on your schedule.

## Pause your subscription

1. Open Account > Subscriptions.
2. Click Pause and choose how long, up to 3 months.
3. The subscription restarts on its own when the pause ends. You can also restart it earlier from the same page.

## Timing

Make changes at least 48 hours before your next ship date. Changes made later apply to the delivery after that, because the bag is already being roasted and packed.

You can see your next ship date at the top of the Subscriptions page.

## Going away for longer?

If you need more than 3 months, you can cancel and start again later. Cancelling has no fee. See "How do I cancel my coffee subscription?".
