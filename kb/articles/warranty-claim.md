title: How do I make a warranty claim?
category: warranty
---

Use the Warranty claim form on the Contact us page. Send your order number, the serial number and a short video of the problem, and we reply within 2 business days.

## What to include

- Your order number. You find it in your confirmation email or in Account > Orders.
- The serial number. See "Where do I find the serial number on my machine or grinder?".
- A short video that shows the problem, for example a pump noise or a leak. A video helps us find the cause faster than a written description.

## What happens next

1. We review the claim and reply within 2 business days.
2. If it's approved, we email you a prepaid shipping label.
3. You send the item to us. Pack it in its original box if you still have it.
4. We repair it or send you a replacement.

You don't pay anything for an approved claim, including shipping.

## Before you send a claim

Some problems have simple fixes. A machine that pulls fast, watery or bitter shots usually needs a grind change, not a repair. See "How to calibrate your grinder for espresso".

If the machine has not been descaled in a while, descale it first and test again.
