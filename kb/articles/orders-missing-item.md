First check whether your order shipped in more than one parcel, because larger orders often do. If an item is still missing, contact us and we will send it.

## Check if your order shipped in parts

Machines and grinders often ship in their own box. When an order ships in parts, you get one tracking email per parcel, and Account > Orders lists every parcel.

## Check the packing slip

The packing slip lists what is in that box. Items marked "ships separately" are in another parcel.

## Small items

Small accessories like gaskets, filters and cleaning tablets can hide in packing paper or inside a machine's box. Check the box carefully before you recycle it.

## How to report a missing item

Contact us with:

- your order number
- the item that is missing
- a photo of the packing slip

We check the warehouse records and send the missing item at no cost to you. You don't need to return anything.

## A part is missing from a machine

If the box arrived but a part is missing, for example the tamper or a filter basket, tell us which part. We send it on its own, so you don't have to send the machine back.
