title: How do Wrenfield gift cards work?
category: payments
---

Wrenfield gift cards are digital codes you can use on any order in the store where you bought them. We email the code to the person you choose, on the date you choose.

## Buying a gift card

1. Open Gift cards in the store menu.
2. Choose an amount, the recipient's email and a delivery date.
3. Add a short message if you like and check out.

## Using a gift card

Enter the code in the payment step at checkout. If the order costs more than the gift card balance, pay the rest with another payment method.

Any balance left stays on the card for your next order.

## Checking the balance

Enter the code in Account > Gift cards to see the balance.

## Rules

- Gift cards can't be returned or exchanged for cash.
- A gift card works only in the store it was bought for, so a US gift card can't be used in the UK store.
- Gift cards can't be used to buy other gift cards.

## Lost code

If the recipient can't find the email, ask them to check their spam folder. If it's not there, contact us with the buyer's order number and we will send it again.
