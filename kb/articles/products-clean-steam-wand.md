title: How to clean the steam wand
category: products
---

Wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth and purge it right after every use. Once a week, soak the tip to clear milk that has dried inside.

## After every use

1. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth right away.
2. Point the wand into the drip tray and open the steam for a couple of seconds to blow out any milk inside.
3. Close the steam and wipe the wand again.

Milk dries fast on a hot wand, so do this before you drink your coffee.

## Once a week

1. Let the wand cool down.
2. Unscrew the steam tip with the small wrench that comes with the machine.
3. Soak the tip in warm water with a little milk cleaner.
4. Clear the holes with the pin from the cleaning kit.
5. Rinse the tip, screw it back on and purge the wand once.

## If the steam is weak

- Check the tip holes for dried milk and clear them.
- If all holes are clear, the machine may need descaling. See "How to descale your espresso machine".

## Safety

The wand gets very hot during use. Hold it by the rubber grip, and never point it at yourself or anyone else when you open the steam.
