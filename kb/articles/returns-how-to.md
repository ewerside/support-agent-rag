title: How do I return an item?
category: returns
---

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Start the return from Account > Orders and we email you a prepaid return label.

## Steps

1. Go to Account > Orders and open the order.
2. Click Return items and choose the items and the reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label from the email we send you.
4. Pack the item, ideally in its original box, and drop it off with the carrier.

If you checked out as a guest, use the link in your order confirmation email to start the return.

## Refunds

The refund goes to your original payment method within 5 business days after the item reaches our warehouse. You get an email when we issue it.

## What to know before you return

- Some items can't be returned. See "Which items can't be returned?".
- Opened espresso machines and grinders have a restocking fee. See "Is there a restocking fee?".
- Empty the water tank and drip tray of a machine before you pack it, and let it dry.

## Returning a gift

If you received the item as a gift, the person who ordered it needs to start the return. The refund goes to their payment method.

## Faulty or damaged items

If an item arrived damaged or stopped working, don't start a normal return. See "My order arrived damaged" or "How do I make a warranty claim?".
