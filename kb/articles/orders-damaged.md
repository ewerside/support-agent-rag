Sorry about that. Contact us with photos of the damage and the box, and we will send a replacement or refund you.

## What to send us

- your order number
- photos of the damaged item
- photos of the outside of the box and the packing
- a short note on what happened, for example a cracked drip tray or a dented case

Photos help us file a claim with the carrier. Keep the box and packing until your case is closed.

## Damaged items and returns

A damaged item is not a normal return. There is no restocking fee and you don't pay for shipping.

If we need the item back, we email you a prepaid label. Often we don't need it back at all.

## If the machine works but the case is scratched

You can keep it with a partial refund or ask for a replacement. Tell us which one you prefer when you contact us.

## If the box looks damaged when it arrives

Take a photo before you open it. If the carrier is still there, ask them to note the damage. Then open the box and check everything, including the glass parts and the drip tray.

## If the machine stops working later

That is a warranty case, not shipping damage. See "How do I make a warranty claim?".
