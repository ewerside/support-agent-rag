title: How do I change my coffee or delivery schedule?
category: subscription
---

Go to Account > Subscriptions and click Edit. You can change the coffee, the grind, the number of bags and how often it arrives.

## Change the coffee or grind

Pick any coffee from the subscription list and choose whole bean or a grind size. The change applies to the next bag that hasn't been packed yet.

## Change how often it arrives

You can get coffee every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. Your next ship date moves to match the new schedule.

## Get more than one bag

Set the quantity to two or more bags per delivery. Each bag gets the same subscription price, and the whole delivery ships free.

## Timing

Make changes at least 48 hours before your next ship date. Changes made later apply to the delivery after that.

## Change the address or card

- Shipping address: Account > Subscriptions > Edit > Shipping address.
- Payment card: Account > Payment methods. The new card is used from the next delivery.

The address has to stay in the same country as your subscription. To move a subscription to another country, cancel it and start a new one in that country's store.
