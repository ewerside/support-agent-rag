id: warranty-wear-parts
title: Are gaskets, burrs and other wear parts covered by the warranty?
category: warranty
---

No. Gaskets, burrs and other wear parts wear out with normal use, so the warranty doesn't cover them. You can buy replacements in the Parts section of our store.

## What counts as a wear part

- Group head gaskets
- Shower screens
- Grinder burrs
- Water filter cartridges
- Steam wand tips

## How to make them last

- Backflush the group head regularly. See "How to backflush your espresso machine".
- Wipe and purge the steam wand after every use. See "How to clean the steam wand".
- Descale on schedule. Scale buildup wears parts faster, and damage from scale isn't covered by the warranty.

## Burrs

Grinder burrs last a long time with home use, but they do get dull. When your grinder takes longer to grind or the grounds look uneven, it is time to replace them. See "How to replace the burrs in your grinder".

## When a wear part fails early

If a gasket or another wear part fails in the first weeks, it may be a defect, not wear. Contact us with a photo or video and we will check it.
