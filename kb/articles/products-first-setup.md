Unpack the machine, rinse the parts, fill the water tank and run water through the group head and steam wand before your first shot. You only need to do this once.

## Unpack

1. Remove all tape and packing inserts, including the foam under the drip tray.
2. Keep the box. You need it if you return the machine or send it in for repair.
3. Check that you have the portafilter, the single and double baskets, the blind basket, the tamper and the water filter.

## Rinse and fill

1. Wash the water tank, baskets and portafilter in warm water.
2. Soak the water filter in water, then fit it into the tank.
3. Fill the tank with fresh, cold water.

## First run

1. Place the machine on a flat, dry surface near a power outlet.
2. Turn it on and wait for the ready light.
3. Put a jug under the group head and run water through it until the flow is steady.
4. Open the steam wand over the drip tray until steam comes out.
5. Empty the drip tray.

## Your first shot

Set your grinder and pull your first shot with the steps in "How to calibrate your grinder for espresso".

## Keep it running well

- Descale every 2 months. See "How to descale your espresso machine".
- Backflush the group head every week. See "How to backflush your espresso machine".
