title: How do I track my order?
category: orders
---

When your order leaves the warehouse, we email you a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also see tracking in Account > Orders.

## When will I get the tracking email?

Orders placed before 1 pm local warehouse time on a business day leave the same day, and the tracking email goes out then. Orders placed later leave on the next business day.

The carrier's page can take a few hours to show the first scan after the email arrives.

## Ask our chat assistant

Our chat assistant can look up your order. Give it your order number, which looks like WCG-10001, and the email you used at checkout.

For your privacy, it only shows the order when both match. If they don't, it can't tell you anything about the order.

## What the order status means

- Processing: we have your order and are packing it.
- Shipped: the parcel is with the carrier and tracking is live.
- Delivered: the carrier marked the parcel as delivered.
- Returned: we received your return.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and refunded.

## I don't see my order or tracking

- Check your spam folder for the confirmation and tracking emails.
- If you checked out as a guest, use the links in those emails instead of Account > Orders.
- If your order is not in Account > Orders, make sure you are signed in with the email you used at checkout.
