title: How much does shipping to Canada cost?
category: shipping
---

Standard shipping to Canada costs C$12.95 and takes 5 to 8 business days. Express shipping costs C$24.95 and takes 2 to 4 business days.

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 5 to 8 business days | C$12.95 |
| Express | 2 to 4 business days | C$24.95 |

Canadian orders ship from our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio, and the delivery time includes customs clearance. Prices in the Canadian store are in Canadian dollars.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over C$100. Express shipping always has a charge.

## Duties and taxes

We collect duties and GST/HST at checkout, so there is nothing to pay on delivery. The carrier won't ask you for a customs payment at the door. See "Will I pay customs or import fees?" for details.

## Which carrier do you use?

We hand Canadian parcels to Canada Post or UPS. Your tracking email tells you which carrier has your order.

## Remote addresses

Delivery to remote areas and the territories can take longer than the times above. The carrier's tracking page shows the most accurate estimate for your address once the parcel is moving.
