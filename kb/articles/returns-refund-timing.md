title: When will I get my refund?
category: returns
---

We issue the refund within 5 business days after your return reaches our warehouse. It goes back to the payment method you used for the order.

## Timeline

1. You drop off the parcel with the carrier.
2. The parcel travels back to our warehouse. The tracking on your return label shows where it is.
3. We check the item and issue the refund within 5 business days.
4. Your bank or card company posts it to your account. This can take a few more days, depending on the bank.

You get an email when we issue the refund.

## How much will I get back?

You get back the price you paid for the item. The original shipping cost is not refunded. If a restocking fee applies, we take it from the refund.

## Where the money goes

- Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay refunds go back to the card.
- PayPal refunds go back to your PayPal account.
- Gift card payments go back to the gift card.

## Refund not showing?

If it has been more than 5 business days since the return reached us and you haven't had an email, contact us with your order number. If you have the email but no money yet, check with your bank first.
