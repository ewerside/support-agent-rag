category: contact

You can reach us by chat on our website or through the form on the Contact us page. We don't offer phone support.

## Chat

Click the chat button at the bottom of any page. Our assistant answers right away, looks up orders and can pass your conversation to a person on the team when you need one.

## Contact us form

Use the form for anything that needs photos, videos or a longer explanation. Pick the topic that fits best so your message reaches the right person:

- Order or delivery
- Returns and refunds
- Warranty claim
- Coffee subscription
- Something else

We reply within 1 business day.

## Talk to a person

Ask the chat assistant to talk to a person at any time. It creates a ticket with a summary of your conversation, so you don't have to explain everything again.

## Opening hours

See "When is customer support open?" for our hours.

## Feedback

We read every message about our products and this help center. Use the Something else topic in the form to send us ideas or complaints.
