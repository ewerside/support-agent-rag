title: How do I change the shipping address on my order?
category: orders
---

You can change the shipping address within 2 hours of placing the order. Go to Account > Orders, open the order and click Edit address.

## Why there is a time limit

After 2 hours the order is with the warehouse and the shipping label is printed, so we can't change the address anymore. Once the parcel is on its way, we can't redirect it either.

## If it's too late

You have two options:

- Contact the carrier with your tracking number. Some carriers let the person receiving the parcel change the delivery place or hold it at a pickup point.
- Let the parcel come back to us. When it reaches our warehouse, we refund the order and you can place it again with the right address.

## Changing the address to another country

You can't move an order to another country, even within the 2 hours. Prices, taxes and the shipping warehouse change by country, so cancel the order and place a new one in the right store.

## Tips for next time

Check the address on the order review page before you pay. Your saved addresses are in Account > Addresses, where you can also set a default.
