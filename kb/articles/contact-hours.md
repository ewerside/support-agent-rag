category: contact
---

Our support team is open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm ET, and Saturday, 9 am to 1 pm ET. We reply to messages within 1 business day.

## Chat

Live chat with our team is available during opening hours. Outside those hours, our chat assistant can still answer common questions and look up orders, and it can pass your question to the team.

## Messages

Send a message from the Contact us page at any time. We reply within 1 business day, and often sooner during opening hours.

## Sundays and holidays

Support is closed on Sundays and on US public holidays. Messages sent then are answered on the next business day.

## UK customers

Our hours are in Eastern Time. For UK customers, the team is online in the afternoon and evening UK time on weekdays.

## What to have ready

If your question is about an order, have your order number ready. For a problem with a machine, the serial number and a short video help us answer faster.
