---

Standard shipping in the US costs $6.95 and takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs $14.95 and takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Shipping options

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | $6.95 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.95 |

Delivery times start when the order leaves our warehouse in Columbus, Ohio, not when you place it. Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting public holidays.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Express shipping always has a charge. See "How do I get free shipping?" for how we count the order total.

## Which carrier do you use?

We use UPS and USPS in the US. The carrier depends on your address and the size of the parcel, and your tracking email tells you which one has it.

## Can you ship to a PO box?

Yes, standard shipping can go to a PO box through USPS. Express shipping needs a street address.

## Do you ship to Alaska, Hawaii and US territories?

We ship to all 50 states with standard shipping. Express may not be offered for some addresses in Alaska and Hawaii, and the checkout only shows the options that work for your address.
