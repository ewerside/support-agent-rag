title: Where do I find the serial number on my machine or grinder?
category: products
---

The serial number is on a sticker on the bottom of your machine or grinder. It starts with WF, followed by letters and digits.

## Espresso machines

Empty the water tank and drip tray, then tilt the machine back gently. The sticker is on the base, next to the power cord.

## Grinders

Remove the hopper first so beans don't spill, then tilt the grinder on its side. The sticker is in the middle of the base.

## Other places to find it

The serial number is also printed on the side of the original box, next to the barcode. If you still have the box, that is the easiest place to read it.

## When you need it

We ask for the serial number when you make a warranty claim or book a repair. It tells us the model and the production batch, so we know which parts to check.

## The sticker is missing or hard to read

Send us a photo of the base and your order number. We can usually find the details from the order.

## Accessories

Accessories such as tampers, scales and milk pitchers don't have a serial number. For those, your order number is enough.
