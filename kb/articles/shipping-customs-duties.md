title: Will I pay customs or import fees?
category: shipping
---

No. You won't pay customs or import fees on delivery in the US, Canada or the UK. How that works depends on where you live.

## United Kingdom

UK prices include 20% VAT, and UK orders ship from our warehouse in Leeds. Since the parcel never crosses a border, there are no import duties and no customs charges.

## Canada

Canadian orders ship from the US, so they go through customs. We collect duties and GST/HST at checkout, so there is nothing to pay on delivery. You see the full amount before you pay.

## United States

US orders don't pay import duties, because they ship from inside the US. Sales tax is added at checkout based on your shipping address.

## What if a carrier asks me to pay a fee?

Don't pay it. Contact us with your order number and a photo of the notice, and we will sort it out with the carrier.

## Do customs slow down delivery?

Only for Canadian orders, and the delivery times we show for Canada already include customs clearance. UK and US orders don't go through customs at all.
